## What Testimonial Hero is genuinely great at

Let me concede the important thing first, because it's true. Testimonial Hero makes some of the best customer videos in this market. Full stop.

They run a global videographer network, so they can film your customer on-site in another country without stinging you for travel, or shoot remotely if that's easier. There's a dedicated project manager, a customer-advocacy strategy session before anyone rolls a camera, and a proper onboarding process. That's real operational muscle, and it shows up in the finished product.

The logos back it up. Through the Case Study Buddy acquisition in March 2024 they picked up serious written-case-study pedigree and names like HubSpot. When a funded marketing team says "we need this to look expensive because it's going in front of an enterprise buyer", Testimonial Hero is a completely sensible answer.

Their customers also talk to real customers. They interview actual people telling actual stories, same as we do. So I'm not going to wave "we use real interviews" around like it's a difference. It isn't. On video craft specifically, they're ahead of me, and I'd rather tell you that than have you find out and stop trusting the rest of this post.

## Where we win: an answer to "why them?", without a department

Here's where the two of us actually diverge, and it isn't on whether the video is real. It's on who the thing is built for, and what your team has to do to get it.

Testimonial Hero is built for a marketing department with an annual budget line. The credit model and the per-story pricing both say so. A remote story alone is $5,750, before you're into the annual tiers. That's a plan you present to a team, not a decision you make on a Tuesday because a customer just told you they love you.

We're built for the founder who had that Tuesday. You tell us which customer. Jessie makes the ask, books the call and handles approvals, so nobody on your team has to chase anyone. Jon runs the one interview. Fourteen days later you get your case study, written for $2,500, or written plus video, social cuts and a carousel for $5,000.

## "But they're the trusted leader with the biggest logos"

They are. And if I were them, I'd say exactly that, because it's the strongest card in the deck and it's genuinely true.

So I'm not going to argue you out of it. If your whole problem is "I need a flagship video that looks like a Super Bowl ad and sits behind a HubSpot-sized logo", go to Testimonial Hero.

But most founders who email me don't have that problem. They have a different one. They had a great sales call, a prospect's champion liked them, and then someone else on the committee asked "why them?" and there was nothing on the site to answer it. That founder doesn't need the most expensive video in the world locked inside an annual plan. They need an answer, live in two weeks, without a year of credits.

## Choose Testimonial Hero if

You're a funded marketing or customer-advocacy team, you need broadcast-grade video as your flagship asset, you want on-site shoots in multiple locations, and an annual credit plan fits how you budget. They're the category leader for a reason. No notes.

You're a B2B founder (SaaS or services) with at least one genuinely happy customer, a deal that involves more than one person deciding, and you'd rather pay once, written or written-plus-video, than commit to a year of credits. You keep the intro and the customer relationship. We handle the rest.
