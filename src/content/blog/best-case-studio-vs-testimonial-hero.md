[Best Case Studio](/) is built for a founder who wants the whole proof set (video and written case study, both testimonials, and a social pack) from one interview, for a fixed [$2,500](/pricing/), in 14 days, with no committee and no annual lock-in. Where Testimonial Hero is built for the enterprise, the Proof Kit is built to be **accessible**: enterprise-grade proof, priced and paced for someone who isn't one.

I run Best Case Studio, so you already know where my bias sits. Every figure below comes from their own site or from public 2026 reporting. ## What Testimonial Hero is genuinely great at

Let me concede the important thing first, because it's true. Testimonial Hero makes the best customer videos in this market. Full stop.

They run a global videographer network, so they can film your customer on-site in another country without stinging you for travel, or shoot remotely if that's easier. There's a dedicated project manager, a customer-advocacy strategy session before anyone rolls a camera, and a proper onboarding process. That's real operational muscle, and it shows up in the finished product. The output looks like something a brand with a budget would be proud to run at the top of a launch page.

The logos back it up. Through the Case Study Buddy acquisition in March 2024 they picked up serious written-case-study pedigree and names like HubSpot. When a funded marketing team says "we need this to look expensive because it's going in front of an enterprise buyer", Testimonial Hero is a completely sensible answer. They've earned the category-leader spot, and the brand trust that comes with it isn't marketing fluff. It's real.

Their customers also talk about real customers. They interview actual people telling actual stories, same as we do. So I'm not going to wave "we use real interviews" around like it's a difference. It isn't. On video craft specifically, they're ahead of me, and I'd rather tell you that than have you find out and stop trusting the rest of this post.

## Where the Proof Kit wins: accessible, complete proof

Here's where the two of us actually diverge, and it isn't on whether the video is real. It's on who the thing is built for.

Testimonial Hero is built for a marketing department with an annual budget line. The credit model tells you that on its own. You commit for a year, you draw down credits, and the entry tier is reported at roughly $6,510/yr climbing to $28,800 and $54,000 as you go up. That's a plan you present to a team, not a decision you make on a Tuesday because a customer just told you they love you.

The Proof Kit is built for the founder who had that Tuesday. One fixed price. [$2,500](/pricing/). One warm intro to your happy customer, and I handle the scheduling, the questions, the awkward pauses, the editing, all of it. Fourteen days from the interview you get the lot back, ready to publish. No credits to budget, no plan to renew, no procurement thread.

And you get more formats, not fewer. Testimonial Hero's engine is video, with written case studies riding along as the sidecar since the acquisition. The Proof Kit runs the other way: one recorded conversation becomes a full video case study, a short video testimonial for your homepage, a written case study built to rank on Google and get cited by AI, a written pull-quote, and a branded social pack (LinkedIn carousel, short clips, a stat card). Every format your buyer might check, from one sitting, for one price.

That's what accessible means here. Not cheap-and-cheerful. It means you don't need a department, an annual budget, or a six-week runway to get proof that stands up. You need one good customer and one intro.

## "But they're the trusted leader with the biggest logos"

They are. And if I were them, I'd say exactly that, because it's the strongest card in the deck and it's genuinely true.

So I'm not going to argue you out of it. If your whole problem is "I need a flagship video that looks like a Super Bowl ad and sits behind a HubSpot-sized logo", go to Testimonial Hero. That's the right call and I'll help you find their contact page.

But most founders who email me don't have that problem. They have a different one. They had a great sales call, the prospect went quiet, and there was nothing on the site to back up the pitch when the buyer went looking. That founder doesn't need the most expensive video in the world locked inside an annual plan. They need proof, in every format, live in two weeks, without a committee signing off on a year of credits. The prestige-video question and the founder-proof question are two different questions, and I only claim to answer the second one better.

If you priced this suite à la carte from specialists you'd pay roughly $3,000+ for the video, $1,750+ for the written case study, $2,000+ for a video testimonial, plus the pull-quote and the social, landing somewhere around $7,000 to $10,000+ across three or four vendors. The Proof Kit bundles all of it from one interview for $2,500. Against a premium annual video plan, that's the accessible option by a wide margin. ## Who should pick which

Pick **Testimonial Hero** if you're a funded marketing or customer-advocacy team, you need broadcast-grade video as your flagship asset, you want on-site shoots in multiple locations, and an annual credit plan fits how you budget. They're the category leader for a reason, and for that brief they're the safe, strong choice. No notes.

Pick **Best Case Studio** if you're a B2B founder (SaaS or services) with at least one genuinely happy customer, you want video *and* written *and* social from one conversation, you want it fast, and you'd rather pay a fixed $2,500 once than commit to a year of credits. You keep the intro, the credit, and the customer relationship. I just handle the production.

And if you're somewhere in between, one honest tie-breaker: count the formats you actually need and count the times you'll buy this year. If the answer is "several premium videos, budgeted annually", that's them. If it's "one complete story, done properly, soon", that's me.
