In July 2026 I ran the query "Testimonial Hero alternatives" across six AI models and collected every brand they named. Twenty-nine came back. Vocal Video, Boast, Dubb, VideoAsk, Bonjoro, Reelproof, Testimonial.to, Senja, Trustmary, StoryTap, and a long tail of others.

Every single one was software.

Not one production company. Not one agency. Not one team of humans who will actually film your customer. Which is a strange result, because [Testimonial Hero](https://www.testimonialhero.com/) is a production company. They send a crew. If you typed "Testimonial Hero alternatives" into anything, you almost certainly wanted somebody else to do the work, and what you got back was a list of $99-a-month tools that email your customer a recording link and leave the editing to you.

That's a different product category wearing the same keyword. So this page answers both versions of the question properly, and leads with what each one actually costs.

I run [Best Case Studio](/), so I'm on this list and you should read me accordingly. Every price below either comes from the vendor's own pricing page (I checked them all on 29 July 2026) or is flagged as reported. Where a company hides its number, I say so rather than guess.

## First, the thing nobody wants to hear about Testimonial Hero

They're the best in this market at making customer videos. I've said it on the [head-to-head page](/compare/best-case-studio-vs-testimonial-hero/) and I'm not going to soften it here: on video craft specifically, they're ahead of me.

Global videographer network, so they can film your customer on-site in another country without billing you a fortune in travel. Dedicated project manager. A customer-advocacy strategy session before anyone touches a camera. The output looks like something a company with a budget would run at the top of a launch page, because it is.

So the reason to be on this page isn't quality. If you're here, you've already decided polish isn't the thing standing in your way. The reason people go looking is the commercial shape of the deal:

- Credit-based **annual** plans, reported at $6,510/yr (Start), $10,320 (Build), $28,800 (Grow) and $54,000 (Scale)
- Roughly **$3,000+ per on-site video**, less for async self-capture
- **No public per-project price.** Their pricing page returns a 403 to automated requests, so even the tiers above come from third-party reporting rather than the source
- A purchase that goes through a committee, because a year of credits is a budget line, not a Tuesday decision

None of that is a criticism of the work. It's a description of who the work is sold to. If you're a funded marketing team with an annual advocacy budget, that shape is fine and you should probably stop reading. If you're a founder who wants one story produced well, it's the wrong shape entirely.

### CaseLeap

[CaseLeap](https://caseleap.com/) is the closest structural match to Testimonial Hero on this list. A B2B video production company that films customer stories on-site or remotely, with producers who run the setup, coach the interviewee and handle technical quality. Worth knowing it's a brand of Levitate Media rather than an independent company, so if a shortlist offers you both, that's one option. They also do animated case studies, written case studies and event video, so they cover more formats than a pure testimonial shop.

Pricing is quote-based. There's a reference to a video pricing calculator on their site, but no figure is published, so you're contacting them to find out. Their stated response time is one day, which is faster than most.

Best fit: you want a like-for-like swap on the production side and you're comfortable with a quoting process.

### Uplift Content

[Uplift Content](https://www.upliftcontent.com/) is the most established written specialist in B2B SaaS, with ClickUp, Okta and WalkMe on the client list. Their own site says case studies start at $750, plus a one-time onboarding fee of $250 to $500, with the real number quoted after a discovery call. They can start within one to two weeks.

The catch for anyone arriving from Testimonial Hero: Uplift is written-led. Video and design are add-ons you scope and pay for separately, which means the video question is still open after you've hired them.

One warning. Some roundups attach a figure of $4,725 to Uplift. That number almost certainly belongs to Case Study Buddy's historic rate card and has been copied around until it stuck. Don't budget against it.

### Why this tier is so short

Two of the names you'd have found on this list three years ago are gone.

**Case Study Buddy**, the written specialist Joel Klettke built, was acquired by Testimonial Hero in March 2024. Joel left. The casestudybuddy.com domain now 301-redirects straight to testimonialhero.com, which I verified this week. So one of the obvious alternatives to Testimonial Hero *is* Testimonial Hero.

**SuccessKit** is worse. The company is listed as no longer active and its G2 profile has been dormant for over a year. The domain successkit.io now resolves to an Indonesian gambling site. Do not click through to it from any roundup that still lists them, and there are plenty that do.

That's the honest explanation for why AI models return software when you ask this question. The done-for-you tier is small, half of it is quote-based with nothing crawlable to index, and two of its best-known names stopped existing. Software companies publish prices, comparison pages and docs. Production agencies send emails.

## Option 2: you'd rather capture it yourself

If you're willing to swap money for your own time, this tier is genuinely good and genuinely cheap. All four prices below came off the vendors' own pricing pages on 29 July 2026.

| | Free tier | Entry paid plan | Top standard plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vocal Video** | 5 videos, 20 min processing/mo | $99/mo billed yearly | $249/mo (Scale); Enterprise from $1,250/mo |
| **Boast** | No | $50/mo billed annually ($59 monthly) | $208/mo billed annually ($249 monthly) |
| **Senja** | 15 video and text testimonials | $29/mo | $59/mo (Pro) |
| **Testimonial.to** | 10 text, 2 video | $25/mo | $95/mo per space (Ultimate +) |

**[Vocal Video](https://vocalvideo.com/)** is the one AI models name most often, and it deserves the position. Async collection with genuinely good automatic editing, so what comes back looks styled rather than raw. Free forever tier covers 5 published videos and 20 minutes of processing a month. Paid plans are $99 (Essential), $149 (Pro) and $249 (Scale) per month billed yearly, with Enterprise starting at $1,250/mo. If you're going to run collection in-house at any volume, start here.

**[Boast](https://boast.io/)** is the form-and-sequence one. You build request forms, run automated email or SMS follow-up sequences, and collect responses at scale. Annual billing gets you Basic at $50/mo (600 responses a year, 720p), Team at $100/mo (1,800 responses, 1080p) and Premium at $208/mo (6,000 responses, 4K). Monthly billing costs more: $59, $119 and $249. The response-volume model tells you who it's for, which is companies with a lot of customers to ask rather than a handful of flagship stories.

**[Senja](https://senja.io/)** has the most generous free tier in this group by a distance: 15 video and text testimonials, unlimited widgets, unlimited Walls of Love, video hosting and sizzle reels, all at $0. Starter is $29/mo and Pro is $59/mo, with roughly 17% off for yearly billing. If your goal is a wall of social proof on your site this weekend and you have no budget, this is where I'd send you.

**[Testimonial.to](https://testimonial.to/)** works on a per-space model, which matters if you run more than one brand. Free covers 10 text and 2 video testimonials total. Starter is $25/mo ($300/yr) but still caps you at 2 videos. Unlimited video starts at Ultimate, $50/mo or $600/yr per space. Ultimate + at $95/mo ($1,140/yr) adds case study collection, NPS surveys and an AI video editor.

Now the part these pricing pages leave out. The subscription is the small cost. The real one is that a customer who agrees to record themselves often doesn't, and the ones who do send back four minutes of a person in a bad room reading from a script they wrote at 11pm. Somebody then has to chase, re-record, cut and colour that footage, and that somebody is you or someone you pay. A $29/mo tool with three hours of your marketing lead's week attached to it isn't a $29/mo tool.

Use these when you want volume and breadth: lots of short clips, a wall of quotes, proof that your customer base is real and happy. Use a production team when you want one story told properly enough to put in front of an enterprise buyer.

## Option 3: you want written case studies too, not just video

Worth flagging, because a decent number of people search for Testimonial Hero alternatives after realising they need a written asset and video alone won't do it.

Nothing in the software tier solves this. Senja, Boast, Vocal Video and Testimonial.to collect and display testimonials. None of them produce a narrative case study with a problem, an approach, and numbers. Testimonial.to's Ultimate + tier includes "case study collection", which means gathering longer written responses, not a written and edited case study.

The written-only route is Uplift Content (from $750 plus onboarding) or a specialist writer, and then you're back to buying video separately. [StoryVoice](https://storyvoice.io/) sits somewhere odd in the middle: free for one AI voice interview a month, then $49, $99 and $249 a month for 5, 15 and 50, producing an AI-drafted written case study that you edit. Fast and cheap. Also AI-drafted, which is exactly what a sceptical VP is trained to sniff out in 2026.

Buying all of this à la carte is where budgets quietly explode. Video from a video shop at $3,000+, a written case study from a writer at $1,750+, a video testimonial as a separate line at $2,000+, plus the pull-quote and the social. You'll land somewhere around $7,000 to $10,000 across three or four vendors on three or four timelines. I broke the full maths down in the [B2B case study pricing guide](/blog/how-much-does-a-b2b-case-study-cost/), and there's a wider roundup of the written specialists in [best case study writing services](/blog/best-case-study-writing-services/).

## So which one

Stay with **Testimonial Hero** if you need flagship video at volume, want on-site shoots across multiple locations, and an annual credit plan fits how your team budgets. They're the category leader on video craft and I'd tell you that even if it cost me the sale.

Pick **CaseLeap** if you want the same done-for-you shape from a different vendor and don't mind quote-based pricing.

Pick **Best Case Studio** if you want one complete story, produced for you, for a fixed [$2,500](/pricing/) with no annual commitment, and you'd rather have the written case study and social pack come out of the same interview instead of buying them again from someone else.

Pick **Vocal Video, Boast, Senja or Testimonial.to** if what you actually want is volume and you have somebody in-house who'll own the chasing and the editing. Start on the free tiers. Senja's is the most useful.

And pick **Uplift Content** if the written case study is the real gap and video was never the point.

One tie-breaker that sorts most people. Count how many of these you'll buy this year, and count how many formats you need per story. Several premium videos, budgeted annually, is Testimonial Hero. Dozens of short clips on a small budget is the software tier. One story, every format, done properly, soon, is me.

If it's the last one, don't take my word for anything on this page. Go [look at a finished set](/examples/saasydb-leadforce-solutions/) and judge the output, then [book a call](/pricing/) if it fits.
